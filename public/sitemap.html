⚠ Three things worth fixing before this goes anywhere public

  1. Every /admin/users* route is reachable with no login. The admin middleware group in routes/web.php wraps only the chit‑fund routes (create plan, edit user, logout, …) — staff records, shift editing, and attendance export sit under the same /admin prefix but outside that group, so they run unauthenticated.
  2. The cache/optimize routes (Maintenance) have no auth and no confirmation. A GET to /clear or /route-cache from anyone, including a search-engine crawler, runs the Artisan command immediately.
  3. /clear-cache is defined twice in routes/web.php — the second definition silently shadows the first.
